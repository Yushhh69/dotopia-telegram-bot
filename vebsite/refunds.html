Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. Digital Membership

Dotopia is a digital membership service. No physical product is shipped.

2. Cancellation

Because access is a digital service, cancellation requests should be made before access is used where possible.

3. Refund Requests

If you believe a payment was made in error, was duplicated, or you were charged but did not receive the purchased access, contact the Dotopia team through Telegram with your payment details so the transaction can be reviewed.

4. Refund Decisions

Approved refunds are processed through the applicable payment system. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account depends on the payment method and banking system.

5. No Automatic Renewal

Membership does not renew automatically. A new payment is required for each additional 3-month period.

6. Contact

Use the Dotopia Telegram bot for refund and payment support.
