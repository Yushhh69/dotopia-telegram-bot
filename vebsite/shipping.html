Shipping Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Digital Service — No Physical Shipping

Dotopia provides a digital membership service. We do not sell or ship physical products, so there are no shipping charges, delivery addresses, courier services, or physical delivery timelines.

Digital Access Delivery

After successful payment verification, membership access instructions are delivered through Telegram. Access is intended to be provided promptly after payment verification, subject to Telegram and payment-service availability.

Access Problems

If you have completed payment but have not received access instructions, contact the Dotopia team through the Telegram bot with your payment details.
