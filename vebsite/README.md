# Dotopia Website

Static landing website for Razorpay business-website verification and customer information.

## Pages
- `/index.html` — landing page
- `/terms.html` — Terms & Conditions
- `/privacy.html` — Privacy Policy
- `/refunds.html` — Cancellation & Refunds
- `/shipping.html` — Shipping Policy
- `/contact.html` — Contact Us

## Before going live
1. Replace or add any business/contact details that Razorpay requires for the actual merchant.
2. Confirm the policy wording matches the client's real refund and membership practices.
3. Deploy the folder to a public HTTPS URL.
4. Submit the live URL in Razorpay Dashboard → Account & Settings → Business website details.

Do not put Razorpay secret keys, Telegram bot tokens, or webhook secrets anywhere in this website.
