To provide membership access, Dotopia may process information such as your Telegram user identifier, Telegram username where available, payment identifiers, and membership dates.

Payment processing is handled by Razorpay. Dotopia does not require or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or banking password.

Information is used to verify payments, activate and manage membership access, send membership notifications, prevent duplicate payment processing, and provide support.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide the membership service, including payment processing and Telegram-based delivery of access.

5. Data Retention

Transaction and membership records may be retained for operational, accounting, security, and legal purposes for as long as reasonably necessary.

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect stored information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Dotopia team through the Telegram bot.
