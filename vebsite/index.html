FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

How do I purchase a membership?

Open the official Dotopia Telegram bot, select your membership and complete payment through Razorpay.

How will I receive my invitation?

After successful payment verification, the bot will send your personal website invitation link.

Can I share my invitation link?

Invitations are designed for individual, one-time redemption and will be associated with the purchasing account.

How long does my membership last?

The standard membership lasts three months. Access is subject to your membership status.

What happens when my membership expires?

Access to premium content will be disabled after your membership expires. You can renew your membership to regain access.

How can I contact support?

Visit our contact page or contact the Dotopia team through the official Telegram bot.
